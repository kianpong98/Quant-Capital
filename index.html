Before you connect an account.

Does this work with any broker?+
Any MT5 broker that allows Expert Advisors and offers fast execution on Gold. Check that your broker's spreads and execution speed suit short-term trading.
What does it trade, and how often?+
Gold (XAUUSD) only. It's a high-frequency engine — expect many small trades per day, each typically closed within seconds to minutes.

No, by design. It deliberately sits out the US session's high-volatility hours rather than trading through them — that's a big part of why the results stay consistent instead of chasing every move.

No. Once connected, the engine scans, enters, and exits trades on its own. You can check in whenever you like.
